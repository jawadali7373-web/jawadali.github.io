Inside the 30 seconds

How I build an ad.

Every reel above follows the same five-beat structure. The timings flex by format, but the order stays.

00:00 – 00:03

Hook

A line or image that stops the thumb before the skip.

00:03 – 00:10

Problem

Name the pain in the viewer's own words.

00:10 – 00:18

Reveal

The product enters as the answer, with a sound cue.

00:18 – 00:25

Proof

Numbers, reviews or a before-and-after that earns trust.

00:25 – 00:30

Call to action

One clear next step, on screen long enough to act on.
